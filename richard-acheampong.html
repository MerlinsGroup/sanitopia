Richard Kojo Acheampong

Founder of Sanitopia. Dual-qualified lawyer, entrepreneur, author, and advocate for community-led sanitation across Africa.

Founder, Sanitopia Projects
Profile

Building dignity, one community at a time.

Richard Kojo Acheampong is the founder and director of Sanitopia, a UK Community Interest Company and registered non-profit in Ghana that builds sanitation facilities for schools and communities across Africa. He launched Sanitopia in 2025 after personally rebuilding the washrooms at his old school, Nkonya Senior High School in Ghana's Oti Region.

Originally from Ghana, Richard moved to the United Kingdom in 1998. Shortly after arriving he was involved in a severe car accident that resulted in the amputation of one of his feet. While still recovering in hospital, he faced repeated deportation threats. Unable to find conventional employment, he started his first business in 2002. Over the next two decades he built multiple ventures that have employed hundreds of people across the UK economy.

Current roles

  • Founder and Director, Sanitopia (UK Community Interest Company)
  • Founder, Sanitopia Ghana (registered non-profit)
  • Practising Solicitor of the Senior Courts of England and Wales
  • Lawyer of the Supreme Court of Ghana

Qualifications

  • Solicitor of the Senior Courts of England and Wales, qualified 2016
  • Lawyer of the Supreme Court of Ghana, dual qualification

Published works

Author of Crushed But Not Destroyed, a memoir of resilience that documents his journey from Ghana to the UK, his recovery from a life-altering accident, and his return to philanthropy. All royalties from the book fund Sanitopia projects in Ghana.

Key milestones

  • 2025: founded Sanitopia following the successful rebuild of sanitation facilities at Nkonya Senior High School
  • Personal investment of more than £44,000 into Sanitopia's first projects
  • 120-day delivery of 37 sanitation units at his alma mater in Ghana's Oti Region
  • Secured partnership with the Ghana Library Authority for nationwide sanitation upgrades
  • Active expansion mission across Ghana scheduled for May 2026

Philosophy

Richard runs Sanitopia like a business, not a charity. Timelines are real. Budgets are tracked. Every project is gate-reviewed and personally overseen. The model is built on the belief that communities deserve infrastructure delivered with the same discipline private investment expects.

Communities that give us everything deserve something back. Sanitation is not charity. It is infrastructure, and it is dignity.

Richard Kojo Acheampong
Founder, Sanitopia Projects
