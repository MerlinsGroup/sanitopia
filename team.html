About / Our Team

The people behind the promise.

A small, distributed team of builders, lawyers, designers and community organisers — bound together by the belief that everyone deserves a clean, safe place to go.

At Sanitopia we are more than builders. We are changemakers working to bring safe, dignified sanitation to communities across Africa, one project at a time.

R
Richard K. Acheampong
Founder & Director

A dual-qualified lawyer of the Supreme Court of Ghana and the Senior Courts of England and Wales, Richard founded Sanitopia in 2025 after rebuilding the washrooms at his old school in Nkonya. He has personally invested over £44,000 into the mission and oversees every project from start to finish.

J
Joan Asare-Acheampong
Co-Founder & Programmes

Joan studied at Nkonya Senior High School alongside Richard. She co-leads Sanitopia's programmes, shaping how every project serves girls, teachers and the wider community.

A
Advisory Board
Strategy & Governance

Our advisory board guides strategy and governance, running the gate reviews and financial controls that keep every project accountable, transparent and built to last.

G
Ghana Operations
Project Delivery

Our Ghana team manages construction on the ground, from site surveys and budgets through to commissioning, working hand in hand with local communities.

L
Legal Counsel
Compliance & CIC Affairs

Our legal counsel keeps Sanitopia compliant across two jurisdictions, handling our UK Community Interest Company and our registered non-profit in Ghana.

C
Community Liaison
Stakeholder Engagement

Our community liaison brings schools, families and partners into every decision, making sure each facility reflects the real needs of the people who use it.
