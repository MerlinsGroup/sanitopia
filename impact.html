Nkonya Senior High School — Our Pilot

37 toilet units delivered in 120 days. We rebuilt the girls' dormitory washroom (12 seats), completed an abandoned 25-unit block, and added a roofed walkway. This was personal — our founders attended this school and saw firsthand how poor sanitation affects students.

Our approach

How we deliver every project.

1. Partner identification

We work with schools, libraries and institutions that demonstrate need and commitment to maintenance.

2. Community engagement

Local leaders, parents, students — everyone has input before we break ground.

3. Local construction

100% Ghanaian workers. Materials sourced in-country. Money stays in the community.

4. Maintenance planning

Caretakers trained, supplies provided, inspection schedules set. Built to last generations.
