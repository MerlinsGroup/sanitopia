Why this work matters

The sanitation gap in Africa's schools.

Across Africa, many public schools lack adequate sanitation, and rural schools are hit hardest. Where toilets and clean water are missing, preventable diseases like cholera, typhoid and hepatitis A spread quickly among students and staff.

Girls carry the heaviest cost. Without privacy or facilities to manage menstrual hygiene, many stay home during their periods, widening the gap in education outcomes over time.

By 2030 we aim to complete at least 1,000 sanitation projects in Ghana, then expand across the continent. Our long-term goal is simple: a clean, safe facility within reach of every child of school-going age.
