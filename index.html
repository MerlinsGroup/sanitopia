Why this matters

Africa's school washrooms are in crisis — we're rewriting the story.

In schools across the continent, toilets stand abandoned: shattered, locked or never finished. The cost is paid in disease, in lost school days, and in the quiet shame of girls who simply stop showing up.

We saw this first-hand at our founders' alma mater. The girls' washroom at Nkonya Senior High School had been unusable for over a decade. So we rebuilt it — properly. And then we promised to do it again, and again, and again.
