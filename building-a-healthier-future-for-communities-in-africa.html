A country-wide partnership

An amazing achievement for Sanitopia CIC. We have signed a country-wide partnership with the Ghana Library Authority to construct 150 modern, dignified sanitation facilities for public libraries across Ghana.

These toilets will serve thousands of children, young people and adults who rely on libraries as safe spaces to read, learn and grow. This collaboration sits at the heart of Sanitopia's commitment to build 20,000 modern sanitation facilities across Africa, ensuring that every child, especially girls, can stay in class with access to safe, private and hygienic toilets.

What this delivers

  • Providing safe, gender-sensitive toilets in schools, libraries and neighbourhoods
  • Protecting public health and reducing preventable disease
  • Keeping girls in education instead of missing school due to lack of facilities
  • Restoring dignity to communities that have been overlooked for decades
