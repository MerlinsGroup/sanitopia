Richard K. Acheampong
The story behind the project

When everything fell apart, he started over.

A car accident. An amputation. Threats of deportation while still in hospital. Years of fighting to find work, then build it from scratch. Richard's memoir is the story of how the human spirit — supported by family, faith and community — can refuse to be defined by what it loses.

Every copy you buy directly funds Sanitopia projects. The pilot at Nkonya SHS was launched, in part, with proceeds from this book.
