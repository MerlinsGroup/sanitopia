# Sanitopia Projects - Website Strategy Brief

## Mission
Building safe, clean sanitation facilities for schools, libraries and communities across Ghana and Africa.

## Urgent Context
Founder travelling to Ghana on 16 May 2025 to close partnerships and donor conversations. Website must support this trip.

## Primary Goals
1. Make visitors understand the sanitation problem
2. Build trust in Sanitopia
3. Show proof of real work
4. Drive action: donate, partner, sponsor, or book meetings

## Target Audiences
1. Individual donors
2. Corporate sponsors
3. Ghana-based partners
4. Public institutions
5. NGOs/development partners
6. Diaspora supporters

## Key Pages (Priority Order)
1. **Homepage** - Clear mission, Ghana focus, donate + partner CTAs
2. **Donate** - What donations fund, giving tiers, sponsor options
3. **Partner in Ghana** - Who we seek, meeting booking, contact details
4. **Impact/Our Work** - Completed projects, proof of delivery
5. **Founder Story** - Richard's personal connection and commitment

## Proof Points (Use Carefully)
- Partnership with Ghana Library Authority
- George Padmore Research Library facility
- Nkonya SHS pilot: 37 units in 120 days
- Founder personal investment: £44,000+
- Registered UK CIC and Ghana NGO

## Core CTAs
- **Primary:** Donate to a Facility
- **Secondary:** Partner With Us in Ghana
- **Supporting:** Sponsor a Facility, Book Meeting, Read Richard's Story, See Our Work

## Tone Guidelines
- Plain, human, serious, credible
- British English
- Avoid charity clichés ("make a difference", "empower", "transform lives")
- Use concrete examples over vague claims
- Keep copy direct and grounded

## What NOT to Do
- Don't invent numbers, dates, costs or beneficiaries
- Don't use generic charity language
- Don't bury CTAs
- Don't add heavy animations
- Don't redesign everything

## Homepage Structure
1. Hero: Mission statement with Ghana focus + primary CTAs
2. Proof strip: Key partnerships and credentials
3. Problem: Sanitation crisis explanation
4. Ghana mission: May 2025 trip context
5. Project proof: Completed facilities
6. Impact model: What each facility provides
7. Donation ladder: Giving tiers
8. Partnership section: Types of partners needed
9. Founder story teaser
10. Final CTA

## Donate Page Must Include
- Where money goes (materials, labour, maintenance)
- Clear giving tiers (£25-£5,000+)
- Major donor/sponsor options
- Trust signals (UK CIC, Ghana NGO, proven delivery)
- Donor updates and evidence promise

## Partner in Ghana Page Must Include
- Types of partners sought
- Support needed
- Why Ghana, why now
- Meeting booking form
- WhatsApp/email contact
- Suitable for sending before/after Ghana meetings

## Development Notes
- Work within existing site structure
- Minimal, clean changes only
- Reuse existing components
- Keep existing strong visuals
- Improve hierarchy and conversion
- Make sections scannable

## Success Metrics
- Clear understanding of sanitation problem
- Trust in Sanitopia's credibility
- Visible proof of completed work
- Clear pathways to donate or partner
- Support for May 2025 Ghana trip

## Key Messaging
"Sanitopia builds modern sanitation facilities through credible local partnerships, starting with Ghana. Every facility protects health, supports education and restores dignity."
