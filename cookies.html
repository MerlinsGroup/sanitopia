Cookies Policy

Last updated May 2026.

This Cookies Policy explains how Sanitopia uses cookies and similar technologies on sanitopiaprojects.org. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites function, remember preferences and gather analytics on how visitors use the site.

How we use cookies

  • Essential cookies are required for the website to operate, such as remembering session information and load balancing.
  • Functional cookies remember preferences such as language or display settings.
  • Performance and analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • Security cookies support fraud prevention and protect against malicious activity.

Cookies we set

  • WordPress session and authentication cookies for administrators
  • Caching cookies used by our site performance tools
  • Analytics cookies that collect anonymous information about page views and visitor behaviour

Third-party cookies

Some pages may include content from third parties, such as embedded videos or social media links. These providers may set their own cookies when you interact with their content. We do not control those cookies. Please review the privacy and cookie notices of each provider for more information.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, or alert you before a cookie is stored. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the website works. Guidance for common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences, Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions

Changes to this policy
