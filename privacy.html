Legal / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated May 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sanitopia collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, donate, sign up for updates or contact us. By using sanitopiaprojects.org you agree to the practices set out below.

Who we are

Sanitopia is a UK Community Interest Company and a registered non-profit in Ghana. We act as the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. You can contact us at info@sanitopiaprojects.org or on +44 7386 454673.

Information we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, telephone number and organisation
  • Donation details, including amount, payment method and Gift Aid status where applicable
  • Correspondence you send us through forms, email or messaging apps
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device and pages viewed
  • Marketing preferences if you choose to receive newsletters or updates

How we use your information

We use your information to process donations and issue acknowledgements, respond to enquiries and partnership requests, send updates about our work where you have asked to receive them, manage and improve our website, and meet legal obligations, including HMRC Gift Aid reporting where relevant.

The legal bases we rely on are your consent, our legitimate interest in running and improving our charitable activities, and compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted partners who help us deliver our work, including payment processors that handle donations, email and hosting providers used to run our website and communications, professional advisers such as auditors and legal counsel, and government bodies where required by law (for example, HMRC). All partners are required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we have agreed.

International transfers

Because we operate in both the United Kingdom and Ghana, some information may move between these jurisdictions. Where data leaves the UK, we apply appropriate safeguards as required under UK GDPR.

How long we keep your information

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes set out above, or to meet legal and regulatory requirements. Donation records, for example, are typically retained for seven years to meet HMRC requirements.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information where there is no good reason to continue processing it
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis for processing

To exercise any of these rights, email info@sanitopiaprojects.org. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies. See our Cookies Policy for details.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's data

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at info@sanitopiaprojects.org or on +44 7386 454673.
